1. **Legal Standards for AOT**: Understand the legal criteria that must be met for the court to grant an AOT order. The court must find that:

   - The individual has a mental illness.
   - The individual is unlikely to survive safely in the community without supervision.
   - The individual has a history of non-compliance with treatment.
   - The individual has a history of violent behavior or is likely to engage in such behavior.
