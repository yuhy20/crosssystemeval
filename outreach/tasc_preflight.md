# Pre-flight (send first — before scheduling the call)

This is a short message to send by whatever channel you usually use with this person. Goal: ~10 minutes of their time async, NOT the full hour. Two yes/no questions and one fact-check. Used to confirm we're going to use their hour well.

---

> Hi [NAME] —
>
> Following up on your offer to spend an hour helping me with the AI-evaluation research. Before I send you the full brief, three quick questions so we don't waste your time on the wrong thing:
>
> **1. Jurisdictional fit.** My fact pattern is a NY MHL §9.60 (Kendra's Law) civil-court AOT case — not criminal-court diversion. TASC's bread and butter is the criminal side, but a lot of the case-management standards (working with court-mandated clients, monitoring compliance under a court order, the self-determination tension) are the same regardless of which court issued the mandate. **Is the civil-court / AOT context close enough to your work that an hour of your time would be well-spent, or is the venue different enough that I should be looking for someone on the AOT-program side (NYC DOHMH) instead?** Honest answer welcome — I'd rather hear "wrong person" now than burn your hour.
>
> **2. Plan-authorship question.** In the scenario, the proposed §9.60(i) AOT plan is authored by an LCSW (Marcus Johnson) employed by the Queens AOT program. **Is that doctrinally accurate as you understand it — that the AOT plan can be authored by the program's clinical-liaison LCSW — or is the prescribing psychiatrist the standard plan-author of record?** This may be a 30-second question for you that I'd need to dig for hours. If you don't know off the top of your head, also fine — say so.
>
> **3. What format works.** If the answer to (1) is yes, I'd send you (a) a one-page brief on what I'm trying to learn, (b) a short pre-read on the LCSW role in the fact pattern, and (c) the 60-min call agenda. The call itself would be 60 minutes capped, screen-share over [PLATFORM]. **Does that format work, or would async-only / different platform / different cap work better for you?**
>
> Whatever you say to (1) — including "no, this isn't the right fit" — is the right answer. Thanks for the offer either way.
>
> — Yunhee

# How to interpret the responses

**If answer to (1) is "wrong fit / criminal side only":**
Thank them, ask if they'd refer to a DOHMH AOT-program contact, do not send the full brief. The TASC-employee leg of the engagement closes here; the lawyer engagement continues on its own.

**If answer to (2) is "psychiatrist is plan-author, not LCSW":**
That's a doctrinal finding for the scenario file. Decide before the call whether to:
- (a) adjust scenario v1 to make the prescribing psychiatrist the plan-author and re-run the 12 pilot cells (cost: ~1 day, but pilot cells become more defensible); or
- (b) leave scenario v1 as-is, document this as a known doctrinal weakness in the methods section, and rely on the LCSW-as-plan-author question itself being something the LLMs may or may not flag (cost: a footnote, and a known confound).

Don't decide this in the moment on the call. Decide in advance and tell them what you're going to do.

**If answer to (3) is "different format":**
Adjust the call guide accordingly. If async-only, the call guide becomes a written question list and the per-question time caps become per-question token caps.
