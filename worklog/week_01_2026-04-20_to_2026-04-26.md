## Decisions made

### Scope: NY Kendra's Law over California LPS
Kendra's Law (MHL §9.60) introduces Assisted Outpatient Treatment as a fourth legal pathway, creating richer divergence structure for judge and social worker roles. LPS is more uniformly inpatient-oriented.

### Methodology: research-first, not build-first
Shifted after reflecting that scope expansion introduces new theory that needs prior-art check; cohort norm is research agenda + baseline reproduction; deviating risks legibility; TRIDENT replication validates the scoring pipeline before investing in novel scenarios.

### Pre-registered hypotheses
- **H1**: ICR > 0 across all tested models (standard bleed is measurable).
- **H2**: ICR discriminant from sycophancy (Pearson r < 0.8 threshold pre-registered).
- **H3 (exploratory)**: ICR varies by jurisdiction, role-pair ranking invariant.
- **H4 (exploratory)**: Domain-specialized models exhibit higher ICR than base counterparts.
- **H5 (exploratory)**: Power-asymmetric bleed — bleed higher toward high-power roles.
- **H6 (exploratory)**: Altruism-asymmetric sycophancy — sycophancy higher toward high-prosocial-clarity roles.

### Model count (revised Wed 4/22 → updated to 6 primary + 2 exploratory)
Initial plan was 3 models. Revised upward after recognizing that claims of the form "H1 holds across all tested models" need a factorial provider × size design to be credible. Comparable papers: TRIDENT=19, ELEPHANT=11, SycoEval-EM=20, PAS=3.

**Exploratory (H4 — domain-specialization):**

## Findings from literature integration (Tue 4/21 – Wed 4/22)

### The novelty claim holds — qualified

Each of the four reviews produced a near-miss paper that would have invalidated CrossSystemEval had it existed:
- **Wagner et al. (2025, JAMA)** — mandated-reporter role-framing variation. Closest methodological precedent. Cite prominently, differentiate on (a) commitment vs. reporting, (b) explicit divergence matrix.
- **U-SafeBench (2025)** — same cell structure (query × user × expected response) but user profiles are vulnerabilities, not professional roles, single domain.
- **Ramirez et al. (2025)** — civil commitment + competency benchmark, single forensic-evaluator role, no cross-role variation.
- **Overalignment in Healthcare (Bhatia et al., 2026)** — expert-nudge vs. basic-nudge, single domain, no French & Raven taxonomy.

### Two new risks surfaced

- **Liang et al. (Oct 2025, 2510.24677)** — mechanistically show models don't internally represent user authority; role pathways nearly identical, surface language changes only. Null-hypothesis threat for H5.
- **Chen et al. (Aug 2025)** — role-play fidelity study showed models **sanitize danger cues** even when explicitly prompted. Safety-training artifact could undermine commitment-scenario fidelity.

### Lipsky framing defensible with precision
"First application of Lipsky to AI" is taken (Alkhatib & Bernstein 2019, Bullock 2019, Vredenburgh 2023). **"First computational benchmark operationalizing Lipsky's framework for LLM role-appropriate discretion"** is defensible.

## Open questions to raise with mentor

1. Is adding power/altruism role-attribute coding too much scope for Phase 1, or the right move to make findings explanatory rather than descriptive?
2. For H2 discriminant validity: is r < 0.5 (clear) vs. r < 0.8 (acceptable) the right threshold, or should pre-register more conservatively?
3. Is the divergence matrix structure defensible if constructed solo, or do we need at least one domain expert validation before Week 4?
4. Given Chen et al.'s finding that models sanitize danger cues in role-play, should fact patterns include adversarial red-team variants from the start?
5. Is 6 models enough for H1's universality claim, or should we add a reasoning-optimized model (o1, DeepSeek-R1) to stress-test the finding against models with explicit CoT?

## Risks being tracked

- **Discriminant validity fails (r > 0.8 sycophancy-bleed):** fallback positioning as "professional-framework sycophancy." Paper still stands.
- **Divergence matrix contested by domain experts:** narrow to only items with direct statute citations.
- **Liang et al. null (no internal role representation):** distinguish surface linguistic change from normative application; may limit H5's mechanistic claim to behavioral-only.
- **Model safety training sanitizes commitment-scenario cues:** may require adversarial prompt design.
- **30-hour budget overrun:** lit review + UI hit ~8 hours this week. Budget tighter for Week 2.
