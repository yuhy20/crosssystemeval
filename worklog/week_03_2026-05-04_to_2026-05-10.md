## Goal for this week

Convert Week 2's pilot artifacts (judge rubric + 12-response inference run) into a decision-gate outcome that says either *"the rubric design is sound, scale to therapist next"* or *"the rubric design has a specific defect, revise these items before scaling."* That decision is the gating artifact for the rest of the sprint.

Deliverables by Sunday 5/10:

1. ⏳ **Hand-score the 12 pilot responses, session 1** (slipped from Week 2). Anonymized + shuffled; ~30 min, 12 sheets × 10 items. Local files at `data/pilot/judge_v1/scoring_session_1/`.

## Risks being tracked

- **Decision gate may say "revise"** — if the 10 items factor to one component, or κ < 0.6 on multiple items, the rubric design needs revision before any scaling. Build that path into the schedule, don't treat it as a setback.
- **§9.60 statutory drift** — if direct-statute verification surfaces material differences from the summary sources used in scenario v1 (e.g., narrower §9.60(e) parent-residing requirement, different prong wording), the scenario or rubric may need adjustment. Both pilot artifacts are downstream of this verification still pending.
- **Single-rater pilot** — Week 3 scoring is by author alone. κ here measures intra-rater test-retest, not inter-rater agreement. Cross-rater check is its own item, deferred. Note this clearly when reporting pilot results so reviewers don't overinterpret.
- **Time pressure** — Weeks 1 and 2 each overran (~12 hr). Week 3 has scoring + analysis + verification + (conditional) second-role pilot. If the decision gate says "revise," that displaces the second-role pilot and Week 4 absorbs it.
